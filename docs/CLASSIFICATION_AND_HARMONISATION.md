# Classification and harmonisation

## Decision order

1. Preserve every provider field and assign a separate research classification.
2. Accept an explicit recognised printmaking family already identified from catalogue text.
3. Apply versioned deterministic rules to title and description.
4. Apply fingerprinted curated rule overrides to unresolved unique texts.
5. Leave the record indeterminate where the text does not support a defensible decision.

## Included shared technique families

- Calcografía: etching, engraving, aquatint, drypoint, mezzotint and related intaglio terms.
- Litografía: lithography and closely related catalogue variants.
- Serigrafía: screenprint, silkscreen and serigraph.
- Digital: giclée, inkjet or an explicit digital-print statement.
- Relieve: woodcut, linocut and woodblock.
- Fotomecánica: photogravure, collotype, offset and related photomechanical processes.

## Exclusions

Photographs, photographic equipment, commercial reproductions, posters, drawings, paintings,
mixed-media unique works, books/documents and objects are excluded. Derivative attributions,
“after” versions and multiwork lots are excluded from the cross-channel comparison.

A zero in `signed_documented` or `edition_documented` means only that the feature was not explicitly
documented in the fields used. It does not establish that the impression was unsigned or unnumbered.
